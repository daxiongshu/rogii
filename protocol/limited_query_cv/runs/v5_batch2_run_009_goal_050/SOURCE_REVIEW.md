## Competition mechanism

The legal query is a complete-well inverse problem.  The trajectory, complete
horizontal GR, paired typewell TVT/GR, and visible horizontal TVT prefix are
available at inference.  Writing

```text
U(MD) = TVT(MD) + Z(MD)
```

shows that `Z` already supplies the high-frequency trajectory wiggle.  Most
remaining error is the lower-frequency geological surface and the phase of the
horizontal/typewell GR alignment.

## Non-duplication

Run 6 trained native-row recurrent refiners against the full residual sequence;
their loss was dominated by high-frequency residual detail and their clean
selector rejected them.  Run 6 also trained categorical and multi-trajectory
path decoders, then tried scalar shares, masks, and per-route utility models.
Run 9 instead makes the low-dimensional estimand explicit: four anchored
Bernstein controls of the smooth residual.  It compares three independent
information routes:

1. a CPU meta-model over 30 immutable trajectory-posterior paths plus several
   selection-clean archived paths;
2. native-row GR/typewell/trajectory sequence encoders with a global control
   head;
3. global alignment-image encoders with the same control target.

The common output basis makes model fusion auditable while the three inputs and
architectures remain materially different.
